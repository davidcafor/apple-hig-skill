# Source registry

Original summaries in this skill are based on official Apple documentation. Local rule IDs belong to this project. They are not Apple rule numbers or quotations. Review dates identify a source review, not a promise that guidance remains unchanged.

The machine-readable [registry](sources.json) records the exact URLs and review scope. The 2026-10-08 review covered the complete platform overviews and Settings page, plus recommendation headings and selected explanatory paragraphs on the other pages. It was not a line-by-line review of every HIG page. Downloaded Apple manuals are not redistributed here.

For version-sensitive APIs, also consult the corresponding current SwiftUI, WidgetKit, ActivityKit, CarPlay, or accessibility API documentation and inspect the project's SDK. A HIG page does not prove an API's deployment availability.

## Maintenance

When changing a rule, open its official source, check the surrounding context and platform exceptions, and update the review date only for sources actually rechecked. Record meaningful changes in the changelog. A reachable URL is not a substantive source review. If Apple removes or reorganizes a page, find its official replacement and reassess the rule rather than just replacing the link.

## Coverage boundary

This is a curated review library, not a complete copy or exhaustive interpretation of the HIG. Specialized areas such as games, HealthKit experiences, Wallet, Apple Pay, Pencil-specific tools, media playback, advanced spatial interaction, and every extension type require additional task-specific official research.
