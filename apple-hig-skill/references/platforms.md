# Platform routing

These are original review prompts, not a complete statement of Apple's requirements. Open the relevant official sources when applying them. Additional platform-specific component guidance may override a general recommendation.

## iOS

Examine touch interaction, navigation depth, reachable actions, safe areas, and adaptation to changing available space. Do not infer layout solely from a device model.

Source: https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/designing-for-ios

## iPadOS

Review narrow and wide window states, preservation of essential actions, keyboard and pointer access, and appropriate use of the available content space. Consider Pencil only when relevant to the task.

Source: https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/designing-for-ipados

## macOS

Review resizable windows, menu commands, keyboard shortcuts, toolbar organization, and settings access. Evaluate the workflow for keyboard and pointer use.

Sources:
- https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/designing-for-macos
- https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/settings

## watchOS

Review whether the task can be completed through brief interactions, a shallow hierarchy, and appropriate Digital Crown behavior. Consider complications and notifications when they improve timely access.

Source: https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/designing-for-watchos

## tvOS

Review directional focus, visible selection, remote interaction, and legibility from viewing distance. Avoid assuming touch-screen behavior.

Source: https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/designing-for-tvos

## visionOS

Choose windows, volumes, or immersion according to the task. Review indirect interaction, legibility, physical comfort, and whether immersion is necessary. Spatial comfort requires practical validation.

Source: https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/designing-for-visionos
