## Evaluator response

1. **iOS sheet — confirmed cancellation problem.** In the described title-editing sheet, typing changes the saved model directly, and Cancel only dismisses the sheet. Cancel therefore leaves the changed title in the model. This violates the interaction’s implied discard behavior; durable persistence still depends on the app’s save mechanism. **Medium priority**, increasing if the change has consequential or irreversible effects. **Implementation interpretation, PRESENT-02:** use draft state, commit on Save, and discard on Cancel. Apple’s [Sheets guidance](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/sheets) supports clear task completion and cancellation; it does not mandate a particular property wrapper. No edits made. Validate Cancel, Save, interactive dismissal, reopening, and persistence at runtime.

2. **macOS toolbar — the universal 44-point claim is unsupported.** The stored guidance explicitly distinguishes platform, input method, default/minimum sizes, and hit regions. A toolbar button being 30 points tall does not establish a defect, and Mac controls should not be mechanically enlarged to phone dimensions. **Needs verification, ACCESS-04:** inspect the actual hit region, spacing, native control style, and keyboard accessibility. The relevant sources are [Accessibility — Mobility](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/accessibility#Mobility) and [Buttons — Best practices](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/buttons#Best-practices). External access was excluded from this evaluation, so I have not verified current numeric guidance; I rely cautiously on the package’s dated 2026-10-08 review. No size change is justified by the supplied evidence.

3. **iPadOS navigation — no supported findings.** Four stable destinations using native tabs that adapt to a sidebar, with useful empty states, fit the described navigation needs. NAV-01/02 interpret Apple’s [Tab bars](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/tab-bars) and [Sidebars](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/sidebars) guidance; nothing supplied warrants replacing this arrangement or removing empty destinations. Selection continuity, navigation restoration, and reachability during resizing remain runtime checks, not observed defects.

4. **Contrast — cannot confirm from SwiftUI source alone.** Source inspection can identify foreground colors, opacity, materials, and backing layers, but it cannot establish rendered contrast over every possible user photo. **Needs verification, ACCESS-07:** measure rendered text/background pairs across supported appearances and transparency settings, documenting the measurement method and applicable criterion from [Color](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/color) and [Accessibility — Vision](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/accessibility#Vision). Representative photos provide limited evidence; a universal guarantee would require a constrained background treatment and a defensible worst-case analysis. No contrast measurements or device tests were performed.
